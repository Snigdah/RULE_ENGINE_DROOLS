Your question — what do source_json and drl_text hold? For a BUILDER rule, source_json is the editable spec the UI saved, and drl_text is generated from it. For a DRL rule, source_json is null and drl_text is hand-written. The engine only ever compiles drl_text.
drl_text — every rule (what the engine runs)
source_json — the builder rules only
